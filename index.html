03Change Communication · Restaff

Saying hard things, kindly

Situation

The company's first-ever 22-person layoff, followed by benefit cuts (loyalty reward removed, company trip scaled down).

What I did

Advised the HR Manager on the approach and co-wrote the CEO's message — honest yet caring; explained the context through townhall and the intranet.

Business link

Handling a hard period well protects trust and employer brand. What we observed: transitions went smoothly, some departing colleagues expressed interest in returning, and no resignations linked to the benefit changes were recorded.

Lesson

Honesty isn't saying everything bluntly — it's saying it the right way, in the right context.

Principles I held
Explain the contextDon't dodgeRespect those who stay and those who leaveRight channel, right timing

Internal documents for this case are kept confidential.
